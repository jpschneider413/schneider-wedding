Our Story

John and Gisele first met in the autumn of 2023 at orientation for the UTSA physics PhD program. At the end of orientation, John asked her to help him find his way back to the parking lot, since he was new to the UTSA campus.

That semester, while enduring doctoral level physics courses, they built a close friendship by supporting one another. Along the way, John even served as Gisele’s unofficial bodyguard during her first TV show appearance in The Chosen.

In the late spring of 2025, Gisele began training with John to improve her running. Motivating and challenging each other to better themselves revealed to them that these qualities formed the foundation for a strong and healthy romantic relationship. John asked Gisele out, and they quickly fell in love.

Over the following year, it became clear that their lifestyles were the perfect catalyst for each other’s goals. Their bond strengthened as they traveled, cooked, exercised, and prayed together. John realized he wanted to share an eternal union and family with Gisele, and proposed to her at the end of a half marathon run around Eisenhower Lake in Dallas. Gisele said yes, and they now look forward to celebrating with the people who have been part of their story and to continuing it together!
